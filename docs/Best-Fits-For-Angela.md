# Best Fits For You (Ranked)

Based on what you actually have going for you: a real GitHub presence (~60 repos),
data-analytics background, and genuine LLM-evaluation experience (Outlier, Oracle,
Hydra, RWS research, rating jobs). That combination is exactly what the expert-tier
platforms want, so skip the mass-crowd shops and aim higher.

## Apply to these first

1. **DataAnnotation.tech** — https://www.dataannotation.tech
   Best effort-to-reward ratio in crowd AI work right now. Coding and reasoning tasks
   suit you. Entry is a paid trial, so getting in IS getting paid.

2. **Alignerr** (Labelbox) — https://www.alignerr.com
   Expert trainer network, higher pay tier, Handshake-style structure. Your repos and
   eval history are the selling points.

3. **Mercor** — https://mercor.com
   Interview-based matching into AI-lab projects. This is the one where a strong
   GitHub link in your profile does the most work for you.

4. **Mindrift** (Toloka) — https://www.mindrift.ai
   Flexible freelance AI-training projects for domain experts. Good steady fill
   between higher-tier gigs.

## Worth applying to, expect a wait or a screen

5. **Surge AI** — https://www.surgehq.ai (selective, strong reputation)
6. **Turing** — https://www.turing.com (dev-leaning, good for your coding side)
7. **Prolific** — https://www.prolific.com (per-study AI experiments, easy to start)

## Long shots that are worth the application anyway

8. **Anthropic** — https://www.anthropic.com/careers (safety / red-team / contract)
9. **OpenAI** — https://openai.com/careers (search "expert" or "AI trainer")

## One practical tip

When you apply, lead with the same line everywhere: self-taught builder, ~60 public
repos in a year, data-analytics background, hands-on LLM evaluation across multiple
platforms. Put your GitHub URL where they can click it. That sentence plus the link is
what moves you out of the generic-applicant pile on the expert-tier sites.
