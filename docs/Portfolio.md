## Honest notes before you send this anywhere

- **Private repos do not count to outside reviewers.** They cannot click 15 of these.
  Several private ones are strong (Compass-Ultra-Pro, freelance-toolkit,
  International_Translator). If you want them to count, either make one or two public,
  add the reviewer as a collaborator, or just describe them in your application text.

- **Two repos look like a template or clone, not original work:** `vscode-docs`
  (Microsoft's VS Code documentation) and `nextjs-boilerplate` (a Vercel starter).
  They are fine to keep, but I left them out of the catalog above so your portfolio
  reads as 100 percent your own. Do not feature them.

- **A few public repos have no description on GitHub.** Adding a one-line description
  to each (especially the games and the agent/dev-ops repos) makes your profile read
  far more professional when a reviewer skims it. Quick win before you apply.
