# AI Freelance & Evaluation Platforms

A working list of platforms for AI evaluation, LLM training, reasoning tasks,
safety/red-team work, and expert data labeling. The kind of work that feels like
Handshake AI and Outlier, not survey filler.

Last built: 2026-06-12

## How to use this folder

- **README.md** (this file): the full list, grouped by category.
- **Best-Fits-For-Angela.md**: a shorter ranked shortlist for your background
  (self-taught builder, ~60 GitHub repos, data-analytics, real eval experience).

A few honest notes up front, because the list this came from oversold some things:

- **Pay numbers vary a lot** and change month to month. Treat any rate you hear as
  a rumor until you see the offer. I have not pinned exact rates here on purpose.
- A couple of "platforms" people pass around are not actually freelance sites
  (Humanloop is a dev tooling company, ARC Prize is a competition). I have kept
  them in but labeled what they really are.
- Invite-only / selective ones are marked so you know going in.

Deliberately left off because you flagged them as not worth it: TELUS International,
RWS/Appen crowd work, DataForce by TransPerfect, OneForma/Pactera.

## Tier 1: Best fit for a builder + analytics + eval background

These reward a real GitHub profile and the ability to reason and write clearly.

- **DataAnnotation.tech** — https://www.dataannotation.tech
  LLM training, coding, and reasoning tasks. Widely reported as one of the better
  payers in crowd AI work. Entry is a paid assessment / trial task.

- **Alignerr** (by Labelbox) — https://www.alignerr.com
  Expert AI trainer network. Domain and coding tasks, higher pay tier, structured
  much like Handshake/Outlier. Application plus skills screening.

- **Mercor** — https://mercor.com
  AI talent marketplace that matches experts to AI-lab projects (training, eval,
  coding). Interview-based onboarding. A strong GitHub profile genuinely helps here.

- **Mindrift** (by Toloka) — https://www.mindrift.ai
  Freelance "AI tutor" / training projects for domain experts, including coding and
  analytics. Flexible, project-based, recurring waves of work.

- **Surge AI** — https://www.surgehq.ai
  High-quality RLHF and evaluation data. Selective, good reputation, rubric-style work.

- **Turing** — https://www.turing.com
  AGI/LLM training plus coding expert work. Dev-leaning, so your repos are an asset.

## Tier 2: Established AI eval / data platforms

- **Scale AI / Outlier / Remotasks** — https://scale.com  /  https://outlier.ai
  You know it. Outlier is Scale's contributor side; quality and pay swing by project.
  Watch for invite-only projects, which tend to pay better.

- **Toloka AI** — https://toloka.ai
  Older crowdsourcing platform that pivoted hard into LLM eval and expert tasks.

- **Invisible Technologies** — https://www.invisible.co
  AI training and "AI workflow" / LLM QA work for labs. More ops-flavored, decent pay.

- **Defined.ai** — https://www.defined.ai
  Speech, text, and LLM data marketplace. Corporate clients, slower onboarding.

- **iMerit** (Ango Hub) — https://imerit.net
  Annotation plus expert tasks. More structured / enterprise.

- **Innodata** — https://innodata.com
  Enterprise AI data prep with expert annotator programs.

- **Sapien AI** — https://www.sapien.io
  Crowd labeling and RLHF, newer player.

- **Pareto.ai** — https://pareto.ai
  Data-labeling startup doing expert and RLHF tasks.

- **Micro1** — https://www.micro1.ai
  AI-driven vetting and recruiting that places experts into AI data work.

- **Clickworker / UHRS (Microsoft)** — https://www.clickworker.com
  Microtask crowd, some AI tasks. Filler-tier pay, but flexible and always-on.

## Tier 3: Fellowship / research / hard-to-get (worth watching)

- **Anthropic** — https://www.anthropic.com/careers
  Safety, red-team, and contractor data roles surface here periodically. Selective.

- **OpenAI** — https://openai.com/careers  (search "expert" / "AI trainer" / contractor)
  Runs expert AI-training and contractor programs. High pay, very selective.
  Note: the old "openai.com/contributions" link floating around is unreliable;
  go through their careers page instead.

- **Prolific** — https://www.prolific.com
  Research-study platform. Beyond surveys it now hosts AI/LLM behavior experiments.
  Pays per study, set per-study rates.

- **ARC Prize** — https://arcprize.org
  Reasoning benchmark with prize money. This is a competition, not steady work,
  but it is squarely in your wheelhouse if you want a shot at a payout.

- **Humanloop** — https://humanloop.com
  Reality check: this is an LLM eval/dev tooling company, not a freelance gig site.
  Worth following for full-time/contract roles, not for picking up tasks.

- **AILab (Scale)** — https://scale.com  (AILab is Scale's research arm)
  Mostly invite-only / contractor-routed through Scale. No clean public signup.

## Quick reality check

The honest landscape in 2026: the money and the better treatment have moved toward
the **expert-matching** platforms (DataAnnotation, Alignerr, Mercor, Mindrift, Surge,
Turing) and away from the old mass-crowd shops. Your profile fits the first group.
Start there.
